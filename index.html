Welcome! This is a forum for scholars who are interested in marking-up Arabic texts for digital publication or for digital databases.

Encode

Mark up Arabic-script texts in TEI XML — manuscripts, periodicals, correspondence, epigraphy.

Publish

Turn markup into searchable digital editions and databases, in Arabic and English.

Collaborate

Share tools, references, and models across projects and institutions.
