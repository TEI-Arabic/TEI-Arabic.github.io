Welcome! This is a forum for scholars who are interested in marking up Arabic-script texts, primarily in Arabic, Persian, and Ottoman Turkish, for digital publication or digital databases.

What we do

Encode

Mark up Arabic-script texts in TEI XML — manuscripts, periodicals, correspondence, epigraphy.

Publish

Turn markup into searchable digital editions and databases, in Arabic, Persian, Ottoman Turkish, and English.

Collaborate

Share tools, references, and models across projects and institutions.

Featured Projects
