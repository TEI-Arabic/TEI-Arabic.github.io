The BADR Project

Read about the project on The Digital Orientalist ↗

Newspaper history, Cairo, 1828–1914

News of Cairo

A TEI XML database of Arabic and Ottoman Turkish articles on Cairo's urban development, drawn from the official Egyptian government journal al-Waqāʾiʿ al-Miṣriyyah (1828–1914). Led by Adam Mestyan (Duke University at the time, later Harvard University), with co-directors Hugh Cayless (Duke University) and Mercedes Volait (CNRS, INHA); researchers include Dr. Sarah Fathallah Garaa (Ain Shams University).

Visit News of Cairo ↗

Arabic periodicals, 1800–1929

Jara'id: A Chronology of Arabic Periodicals

A searchable database of more than 3,300 Arabic periodicals published between 1800 and 1929, with dates, places of publication, editors, and holding institutions, in parallel English and Arabic listings. Curated by Adam Mestyan; supported by Till Grallert and Hugh Cayless.

Visit Jara'id ↗

Epigraphy & translation, TEI/EpiDoc

Islamic Funerary Stelae and Arabic Translations of Ancient Sources

Nicolas Souchon (CNRS) works on a TEI/EpiDoc database of Islamic funerary stelae in Cairo, alongside TEI/EpiDoc encoding of Arabic translations of ancient sources.
